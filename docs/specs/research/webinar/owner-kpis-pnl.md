> Research report (webinar review), gathered 2026-09-28. Items marked UNVERIFIED were not confirmed against a primary source.

## Verdict: BUILD-LITE, an "Owner P&L pulse" (monthly, import-first, secondary to the action ledger)

- **Build:** a monthly P&L import that uses AI to map accounts to USALI with human confirmation. Output GOP, GOP margin, GOPPAR, TRevPAR, flow-through and labour %. Add a weekly **Net RevPAR** built only from PMS and OTA data Clefs already has.
- **Don't build:** a GL, a budgeting module, live accounting sync for every system at launch, or the webinar's "+10/+25/+50% GOPPAR" milestone tiers. Those tiers are coach branding, not USALI concepts.
- **Why lite:** working out GOPPAR is commodity arithmetic. The defensible part is tying P&L lines to verified actions in the Clefs ledger (OTA leakage recovered, recovery comps, amenity cost). The other defensible asset is a mapping memory for UAE and India charts of accounts.
- **Timing risk:** UAE hotel profits collapsed in 2026 (below). A "GOPPAR growth" promise is dangerous in 2026 and misleading in 2027, when the market recovers from a low base.

## 1. USALI definitions: standard vs non-standard

USALI 12th Revised Edition is effective **1 Jan 2026** ([Hotel Online](https://www.hotel-online.com/news/usali-12th-revised-edition-is-your-finance-team-ready-for-january-2026)). One source dates its release to Summer 2025 ([Hospitality Upgrade](https://www.hospitalityupgrade.com/magazine/winter-2024/the-usali-12th-revised-edition-an-expert-overview-of-updates)). The book costs $129 for members and $149 for others, and includes a searchable Revenue & Expense Dictionary ([HFTP/HITEC](https://www.hitec.org/news/4125312/hftp-announces-availability-of-usali-12th-revised-edition-soft-cover-book), [usali.hftp.org](https://usali.hftp.org/)). I could not read the primary USALI text because HFTP returns 403. The structure below comes from secondary sources, so buy the book before shipping any formulas.

**Summary Operating Statement** ([Hospitality Finance Network](https://hospitalityfinancenetwork.com/usali-pl-structure-summary-operating-statement-a-practical-guide/)):

| Line | Definition | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Total Operating Revenue | Rooms + F&B + Other Operated Depts + Misc Income | Standard |
| Departmental profit | Dept revenue − cost of sales − labour & related − other expenses | Standard |
| Undistributed opex | A&G (Sch 5), Info & Telecom (6), Sales & Marketing (7), POM (8), **Energy, Water & Waste** (9; renamed from Utilities in the 12th ed.) | Standard |
| **GOP** | Total departmental income − undistributed opex | Standard |
| Mgmt fees (Sch 10), Non-operating income & expenses (Sch 11) such as rent, property tax, insurance | Below GOP | Standard |
| **EBITDA** | After mgmt fees and non-operating items. The 11th ed. renamed "Net Operating Income" to EBITDA ([HotStats](https://www.hotstats.com/hotel-industry-resources/uniform-system-of-accounts-for-the-lodging-industry-usali-10th-vs-11th-edition)) | Standard |
| GOPPAR | GOP ÷ available room-nights ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GOPPAR)) | Industry-standard KPI. Whether USALI defines it is UNVERIFIED |
| TRevPAR | Total operating revenue ÷ available room-nights ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TRevPAR)) | Industry-standard |
| RevPAR / ADR / Occupancy | Rooms rev ÷ available; rooms rev ÷ sold; sold ÷ available ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RevPAR)) | Standard. India's "ARR" means the same as ADR (UNVERIFIED citation) |
| Labour cost %, FTE | The 11th ed. has a labour cost schedule ([HotStats](https://www.hotstats.com/hotel-industry-resources/uniform-system-of-accounts-for-the-lodging-industry-usali-10th-vs-11th-edition)). The 12th ed. adds a **mandatory FTE schedule** of hours per department ([Hospitality Upgrade](https://www.hospitalityupgrade.com/magazine/winter-2024/the-usali-12th-revised-edition-an-expert-overview-of-updates)) | Standard |
| CPOR | Departmental ratios exist in USALI. The exact name and formula are UNVERIFIED | Semi-standard |
| "GOPPAR margin" | Not a standard term. The margin is **GOP ÷ Total Operating Revenue** | Non-standard |

Other 12th-edition changes relevant to Clefs:
- Loyalty programme cost lines.
- Three digital S&M lines: Paid Search, Display, Social.
- A Brand/Operator cost schedule.
- An all-inclusive section.

All from [Hospitality Upgrade](https://www.hospitalityupgrade.com/magazine/winter-2024/the-usali-12th-revised-edition-an-expert-overview-of-updates).

**Revenue normalisation rules Clefs must apply:**
- **OTA merchant model:** book revenue at the gross guest price and record the OTA margin as commission expense ([Revenue Hub on ASC 606/USALI](https://revenue-hub.com/ota-new-accounting-standards/)). Many small hotels book the net payout, which understates both revenue and distribution cost.
- **Dubai folio charges:** a Dubai bill carries 7% municipality fee, 10% service charge, 5% VAT and a Tourism Dirham of AED 7–20 per room-night. The Dirham is collected on the government's behalf ([TaxAdvisors](https://taxadvisors.ae/guides/hotel-hospitality-tax-uae/), secondary). Exclude pass-through items from revenue. How to treat the 10% service charge is UNVERIFIED, so flag it for accountant review.

## 2. Benchmarks

**UAE 2026 market shock (drives everything else):**
- Dubai H1 2026 occupancy was **56.4% vs 81%** in H1 2025. Dubai RevPAR fell **35.2%** and UAE RevPAR fell **31.8%**. Recovery is unlikely before early 2027 ([Skift, 11 Aug 2026, citing CBRE/CoStar](https://skift.com/2026/08/11/uaes-hotel-divide-abu-dhabi-holds-up-while-dubai-sinks/)). JLL cites "regional tensions" and heavy discounting ([JLL Q2 2026](https://www.jll.com/en-ae/insights/market-dynamics/uae-hospitality)).
- HotStats (now part of Duetto), UAE year to July 2026: **TRevPAR −33.5%, GOPPAR −47.8%**. Across the Gulf, each 1% fall in TRevPAR produces roughly a 1.5–1.7% fall in GOPPAR ([Duetto/HotStats, Sep 2026](https://www.duettocloud.com/en-us/library/hotel-budget-season-2027-a-global-snapshot)).
- Pre-shock: UAE RevPAR was +11.9% year to Aug 2025 ([Knight Frank](https://www.knightfrank.ae/newsroom/article/2025/10/uae-hospitality-market-review-2025)).

**GOP margins:**
- Rolling 12 months to July 2026: US 37.2%, APAC 34.6%, Western Europe 33.6%, Southern Europe 41.2% ([Duetto/HotStats](https://www.duettocloud.com/en-us/library/hotel-budget-season-2027-a-global-snapshot)).
- MENA, Oct 2019: GOP margin 38.7%; payroll PAR $53.99 against TRevPAR $203.30, so labour is about 27% of revenue (my calculation) ([HotStats 2019](https://www.hotstats.com/hotel-industry-trends/profit-streak-continues-at-mena-hotels)).
- The "Middle East 44% GOP margin, 2025" figure appears only in a search snippet (UNVERIFIED).
- India:
  - FHRAI survey for FY2017-18: 5-star deluxe GOP 39.6%, 4-star above 30% (secondary snippet about a [CoStar](https://www.costar.com/article/1841185350/india-hotel-survey-shares-performance-profitability) article; the page returned 403).
  - ICRA FY2026 operating margin of 34–36% for large hotel companies ([AxisRooms](https://www.axisrooms.com/blog/hotel-business-profit-margin-india/), secondary).
  - GST on rooms ≤ ₹7,500 dropped to 5% but without input tax credit, which compresses margins ([HVS](https://www.hvs.com/article/10354-indian-hotel-sector-2025-in-review)).
- **UNVERIFIED:** GOP margins for economy and midscale independents in the UAE or India, and UAE utilities cost share. No public source found; HotStats and STR samples are mostly branded hotels.

**Distribution:**
- Cloudbeds' **2026 State of Independent Hotels** report (25 Mar 2026; 90M bookings, 180 countries): OTA share was **63.4% in 2025**, up from 61.3%. OTA cancellation rate was 21.8% vs 10.6% for direct ([Cloudbeds](https://www.cloudbeds.com/articles/2026-hotels-report-reveal/), [Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4131619/cloudbeds-2026-state-of-independent-hotels-report-reveals-tightening-margins-shifting-traveler-behavior-and-rising-ota-dominance)).
- **"36.6% direct" is a derived number, not a published one.** The report does not state that OTA plus direct equals 100%. The 2025 edition only said "61% OTA" ([Cloudbeds 2025](https://www.cloudbeds.com/articles/2025-independent-lodging-report/)).
- UAE-specific direct share: UNVERIFIED.
- Commission: Booking.com typically charges 10–25% (about 15% average) and Expedia 10–30%; actual rates are set per contract ([Zeevou](https://zeevou.com/compare/booking-com-vs-expedia/), secondary). UAE-specific rates are UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Minimal import schema (monthly cadence)

**Tables:**
- `pl_import_batch`: property_id, period_month, source (upload / xero / qbo / zoho / tally_xlsx), file_hash, currency, basis (accrual or cash), status.
- `coa_account`: property_id, source_account_code, name_raw (English, Arabic or Tally ledger name), parent_group, type.
- `usali_map` (versioned): account_id → `usali_schedule` (1–11), `usali_line`, `dept` (rooms / F&B / other / misc / A&G / I&TS / S&M / POM / EWW / mgmt_fee / non_operating / **excluded**), `is_pass_through_tax`, `ai_confidence`, `ai_rationale`, `confirmed_by`, `confirmed_at`.
- `pl_line`: batch_id, account_id, amount_signed.
- `stat_input`: available room-nights and rooms sold (auto-filled from PMS), FTE hours (optional).

**Mapping flow:**
1. An LLM proposes the USALI mapping from account name, group and history.
2. Anything below the confidence threshold, and any new account, goes to the owner or accountant for one-tap confirmation over WhatsApp or web.
3. Confirmed mappings are locked and reused every month.

**Checks before publishing:**
- The mapped total must reconcile to the source P&L net profit.
- Flag if P&L rooms revenue differs from PMS rooms revenue by more than 2%.
- Flag if OTA revenue looks booked net (commission line near zero while OTA share is high).
- Route depreciation, interest, owner drawings and rent below GOP.
- Flag labour or EWW ratios outside set bands.

**What Clefs can compute without a P&L:**
- Rooms revenue, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR (PMS).
- OTA commission and payout leakage (Profit Guard).
- **Net RevPAR**.
- Cancellation cost.
- Amenity and service-recovery cost.
- Task-level labour hours from Service Control (hours only, not payroll cost).

**What needs the P&L:** payroll in currency, F&B cost of sales, A&G, S&M, POM, EWW, GOP, GOPPAR, EBITDA, labour %.

## 5. Do these owners track GOPPAR, and what to show instead

**Evidence:** I found **no survey data** on how many owners of 30–150-room independents track GOPPAR (UNVERIFIED). Vendor content says GOPPAR has become the metric of record at owner level ([RevPARGenius](https://revpargenius.com/insights/revpar-goppar-rgi-mpi-hotel-metrics)), but that is opinion, not data.

**Better weekly metric for owners, available without a P&L:**
- **NRevPAR = (rooms revenue − distribution costs) ÷ available rooms** ([Dataria](https://www.dataria.com/en/glossary/netrevpar-nrevpar/)).
- Kalibri's version, **COPE RevPAR**, also subtracts brand fees, loyalty and marketing tied to bookings ([Kalibri](https://www.kalibrilabs.com/beyond-revpar-how-cope-revpar-impacts-asset-value/)).
- Brand "profit per available room after distribution" as NRevPAR. It fits OTA Profit Guard directly.

**Monthly metrics once a P&L is imported:** GOP, GOP margin, GOPPAR, and **flow-through** (ΔGOP ÷ ΔTotal Revenue). Flow-through is the one that matters in a market with 1.5–1.7x operating leverage.

## 6. BUILD-LITE scope: Owner P&L pulse

1. **Weekly, no P&L needed:** NRevPAR, OTA cost per occupied room, cancellation leakage, service-recovery cost, and actions from the ledger.
2. **Monthly:** upload a P&L or trial balance (XLSX/CSV/PDF) or use a Xero/QBO connector. Zoho comes next; Tally stays upload-only. Owner gets a one-page brief: GOP, margin, GOPPAR, TRevPAR, flow-through, labour %, top 3 cost anomalies, each linked to a proposed action.
3. **Ask Clefs** only over *confirmed* mappings.
4. **Excluded:** budgeting, forecasting, consolidation, and writing back to accounting systems.

**Using GOPPAR growth as a pilot / ROI metric honestly:**
- **Never claim "+25% GOPPAR from Clefs."** UAE GOPPAR fell 47.8% in 2026. In 2027, recovery from that low base could produce a "+50%" year-on-year gain with no product effect at all ([Duetto/HotStats](https://www.duettocloud.com/en-us/library/hotel-budget-season-2027-a-global-snapshot)).
- **Headline ROI** should be "Clefs-verified profit": the sum of ledger actions that were detected, approved, executed and verified (recovered payout, commission avoided, comp cost avoided), in AED per available room.
- **Show GOPPAR only as context,** indexed against the market. Use Demand Radar comp-set rates and CoStar/HotStats market indices, a difference-in-differences comparison against a matched period, and seasonality adjustment for Dubai summers.
- **Pre-register the baseline:** 12 months of trailing P&L plus the confirmed mapping, before the pilot starts.

**Commoditised vs defensible:**
- **Commoditised:** the GOPPAR arithmetic, P&L dashboards (Zoho Analytics already ships one, [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/analytics/creating-profit-and-loss-report.html)), and PMS BI tools.
- **Defensible:**
  - Tying each P&L line to verified ledger actions.
  - Normalising OTA gross vs net revenue using payout data only Clefs has.
  - A mapping memory for Arabic/English/Tally charts of accounts across properties.
  - A benchmark cohort of 30–150-room independents in the UAE and India, which HotStats does not cover well (UNVERIFIED).
- **Constraint:** Xero's terms prohibit training AI/ML models on its API data ([Xero](https://developer.xero.com/pricing)). Keep the mapping-memory learning out of Xero-sourced data unless legal review clears it.
