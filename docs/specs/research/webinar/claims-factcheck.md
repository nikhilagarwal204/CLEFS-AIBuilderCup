## Presenter's rank checker

- **Product:** "Rank My Hotel" ([rank.hotelgrowthhub.com](https://rank.hotelgrowthhub.com)) does daily Google organic and Google Maps rank checks for keywords, with history charts and "reviews needed to rank up" gap analysis.
- **Pricing:** free (3 one-time checks), Pro ₹9,999/yr (100 checks/month plus an AI rank-check add-on), and ₹5,000/yr per extra property.
- **Claims:** the rank-checker page makes no outcome claims. The parent site ([hotelgrowthhub.com](https://hotelgrowthhub.com/)), run by founder Arunkumar Raman, shows testimonials such as "#41 to #1 in 13 days" and "350 reviews in single month". It says it has coached 5,000+ owners and has 500+ paid members. None of these are independently verifiable.
- **Policy risk:** fast review surges are a red flag. Google prohibits incentives for reviews and "selectively solicit[ing] positive reviews", and says merchants "should not… pressure users… while on the premises" ([Google contribution policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114)).

## Recommendation

**1. Marketing stats: BUILD-LITE as a "Claims Registry", enforced in CI.**
- Store each claim with these fields: `claim_id, exact_quote, value, unit, geography, population, period, publisher, source_type (primary|secondary), url, retrieved_at, review_by, status (VERIFIED|SECONDARY|UNVERIFIED|BANNED), allowed_surfaces`.
- Add a site lint that fails the build when any `%`, `AED`, `$` or `₹` number is not tagged with a `claim_id`. Give each claim an expiry, because WTTC restates figures every year and the 2025 and 2026 India figures already differ.
- **Allowed now:**
  - Cloudbeds: 63.4% OTA / 36.6% direct and 21.8% vs 10.6% cancellations, labelled "independent hotels globally".
  - WTTC's Middle East −14.5% for 2026 and the UAE's 11.9% of GDP.
  - JLL's UAE RevPAR −31.8% YTD June 2026.
  - DET 2025 inventory.
  - Skyscanner UAE 81% AI confidence.
  - Booking.com 67% "used AI in some aspect of travel".
- **Banned:** items 4, 7 and 10. India macro figures are irrelevant to a UAE launch; keep them for the India launch. The AI market-size figure is a vanity number that owners don't care about.

**2. Positioning: lean in.** The 2026 UAE downturn (RevPAR down about 32–35%) supports OTA Profit Guard and margin control directly. Growth and labour-shortage narratives from India don't transfer.

**3. Defensibility: the stats are commoditized, and so is rank tracking.** There is no public UAE direct vs OTA split for independents. The "Audit one OTA statement" lead magnet can produce Clefs' own dataset: aggregated, anonymized, consented under PDPL, published with n and methodology once the sample is big enough. That would be a **"UAE Independent Hotel OTA Leakage Index"**, a proprietary stat nobody else can cite. BUILD this.

**4. Rank checker: BUILD-LITE, not a product.** Clefs already uses SerpApi, so a weekly Google Maps local-rank check for 3–5 keywords can feed the owner brief and Reviews-lite as one visibility KPI. It's a commodity feature (SerpApi Maps endpoint pricing not verified here), so don't market it. REJECT review-velocity or "rank #1" promises because of the Google policy risk above.
